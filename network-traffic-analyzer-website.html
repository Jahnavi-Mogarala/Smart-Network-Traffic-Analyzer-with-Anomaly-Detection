About

Detection rules

Packet rate (median + MAD), distinct ports per source and target in 10 s, SYN-only bursts, DNS rate and unusually long DNS names. Thresholds are configurable.

Limitations

Alerts are statistical flags, not verified incidents. Short captures give weak baselines, and legitimate scanners or backups can trigger alerts.

Privacy

Files stay in your browser and are not stored or sent. Only headers are read, never payloads.
